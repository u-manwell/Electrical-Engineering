# Electrical-Engineering
Compilations of problems found in Circuit Analysis I &amp; II coursework, and more...

As of December 2025, this is currently a small scale project inspired by what I would have liked to have during my first and second-year studies at Columbia University as an EE major.
My area of interest consists of analog devices and circuits, which is mostly the content which I will cover. However, I look forward to having a mix of Signal & System, E&M, ODEs, and Linear Algebra content on the way. As I am just an undergrad and refuse to (accidently) spread misinformation, I will restrict the content to purely problems and solutions. However, I do intend to push out theory in the future only after I intensively proofreading my drafts.
If any discrepency is found in my solutions (or would like to make suggestions) please email mpg2169@columbia.edu with the subject line [Github_EE].
