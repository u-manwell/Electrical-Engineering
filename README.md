# Electrical-Engineering
Compilations of problems found in Circuit Analysis I &amp; II coursework, and more...

As of December 2025, this is currently a small scale project inspired by what I would have liked to have during my first and second-year studies at Columbia University as an EE major.
My area of interest consists of analog devices and circuits, which is mostly the content which I will cover. However, I look forward to having a mix of Signal & System, E&M, ODEs, and Linear Algebra content on the way.
